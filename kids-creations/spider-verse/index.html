Dr. Alyssa's Spider Verse

A reality for shifting. Everyone who goes says the first night is the worst.

  1. There are smelly porta potties everywhere.
  2. Everyone is a human with a spider body and a human head.
  3. It's always night, with a red sky.
  4. The fridge is keeping flies cold.
  5. The world has zombie villages everywhere too.
  6. Your own body is a spider body.
  7. The whole world smells like poop here.
  8. There are snakes everywhere too.
  9. The snakes are 3-headed.

You will stay exactly 1 year, and you will return to 1 second after you shifted.

Once you shift, there is no going back early. 365 nights. No sunrise.
