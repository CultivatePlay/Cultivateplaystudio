What makes it different

🤝

Plan it together

The child chooses play-based activities, the order and the pictures. Add photos of the real swing or the real playroom.

🕒

Time you can see

Each activity sits on a real analog clock exactly where the minute hand will be, so "15 minutes" becomes something to watch.

💬

Check in, then move on

When time is up, the app asks first: more time, ready for next, or change the plan. The child's voice shapes the session.

A look inside

Made together, not for compliance

WePlan Clock was created by the pediatric therapists at Cultivate Play Pediatric Therapy. A schedule should help a child know what's coming and trust that they have a say. It isn't a tool to make a child obey.

  • Supports predictability and smoother transitions
  • Builds time awareness, estimating and flexible planning, all parts of executive functioning
  • Copies a session summary for therapists' treatment notes
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on the device.
