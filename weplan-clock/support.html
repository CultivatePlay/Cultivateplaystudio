WePlan Clock Support

Need a hand? Email hello@cultivateplaystudio.com and we'll get back to you.

How do the wedges line up with the clock?

In My hour, each activity's wedge sits where the minute hand will be while you do it, so one hour fills the whole face. In My day, wedges follow the hour hand, so the face is 12 hours. Starting "at this time" lines the wedges up with when you really started.

What happens when an activity's time is up?

The app checks in before moving on: the child can ask for more time, say they're ready for the next activity, or change the plan. Tapping "Done, next!" moves on right away. Extra time comes from free time first, then evenly from the activities still to come, so the session still ends on time.

What's in WePlan Clock Plus?

A one-time purchase that adds My day, My week, unlimited saved plans, child profiles and recorded voices. Planning with a child (My hour, photos, picture symbols, check-ins and changing the plan) is always free.

I bought Plus. How do I get it on a new device?

Open WePlan Clock, tap any 🔒 feature, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Where are my plans and photos saved?

Only on the device you made them on. They aren't backed up to our servers, because we don't collect them at all. See the privacy policy.

Recording a voice doesn't work

Check that WePlan Clock is allowed to use the microphone in your device's settings. You can also upload a voice memo instead.

I don't get alerts when the screen is off

Allow notifications for WePlan Clock in your device's settings. During a session, the app keeps the screen on so the clock stays visible.
