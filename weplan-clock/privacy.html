Privacy Policy

WePlan Clock does not collect, sell or share personal information. Everything you make in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Plans, activity names, child profile names, photos, voice recordings, saved plans, your week, settings and session summaries are stored only in the app on your device (or in your web browser, if you use the website version). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party data collection.
  • No contact with other apps or websites about how you use WePlan Clock.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to add a picture to an activity.
  • Microphone: only when you choose to record a voice for an activity (WePlan Clock Plus).
  • Notifications: to let you know when an activity's time is up while the app is in the background.

You can turn any of these off in your device's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases

WePlan Clock Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple or Google whether Plus has been purchased on your account, so it can unlock.

Children's privacy

WePlan Clock is designed for adults to use together with children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads, and purchases ask a grown-up first. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Session summaries

The "Copy summary for notes" feature puts text on your device's clipboard. It goes only where you paste it.

The website version

The website version of WePlan Clock works the same way and stores everything in your browser. Like any website, the service that hosts cultivateplaystudio.com (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation. We do not use those logs to identify or track anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
