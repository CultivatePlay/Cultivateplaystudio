## Shared description (Apple and Google)

```
WePlan Clock turns an hour, a day or a week into a visual schedule you build together with your child, then shows it on a real analog clock.

Every activity becomes a color wedge right where the minute hand will sweep, so time stops being invisible. Your child can see how long swing lasts, what comes next and how much time is left.

MADE TOGETHER, NOT FOR COMPLIANCE
WePlan Clock was created by pediatric therapists at Cultivate Play Pediatric Therapy. The schedule is a shared map, not a tool to make a child obey. It supports predictability, gives the child a real voice in the plan, and builds time awareness, which is a key part of executive functioning.

PLAN IT TOGETHER
• Choose from play-based activities like swing, obstacle course, pretend play and bubbles, or type your own
• Add photos of real places and things, or pick picture symbols
• Drag activities into the order you agree on
• Pick colors, and tap or type the minutes for each one
• Split the time evenly with one tap

SEE TIME ON A REAL CLOCK
• Start now, or at any time: the wedges line up with the real clock
• Now and Next cards with pictures and a progress bar
• A gentle chime, flash and optional spoken announcement at each change
• The screen stays on during a session

CHECK IN AND CHANGE THE PLAN
• When time is up, WePlan Clock checks in before moving on: more time, ready for the next activity, or change the plan
• Extra time is shared evenly by the activities still to come, so you still finish on time
• Finished early? Add another activity, or keep it as extra play time
• Change the plan together while the clock keeps running

FOR THERAPISTS
• Copy a session summary for your treatment notes: actual times, check-ins, extra time asked for, and activities finished early
• Supports time awareness, estimating, planning and flexible thinking
• Practical guidance on co-creating schedules is built into the app

WEPLAN CLOCK PLUS (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
• My day: plan a whole day on the hour hand
• My week: choose a plan for each day of the week
• Child profiles, so each child or client has their own plans, pictures and voices
• Recorded voices: "Time for swing!" in your voice or the child's
• Unlimited saved plans

Planning together is always free: My hour, photos, picture symbols, check-ins and changing the plan. No subscription. No ads.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Plans, photos and voices stay on your device.
```
